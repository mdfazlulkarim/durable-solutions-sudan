Women and girls

Women and girls carry the heaviest burden of Sudan's war, and they are already leading much of the response. This page brings the evidence together: what women and girls face, where funding falls short, why investing in women pays off for recovery and development, and who is doing what.

The funding gap

Needs have grown faster than funding. Services for women and girls are often the first to close when money runs short.

Why investing in women pays off

Women are not only among those most in need. They keep families fed, run local services and hold communities together, which is where recovery and development start.
